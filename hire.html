Scarifier, Dethatcher & Verti-Mower Hire

Thatch is the spongy brown layer of runners and dead material that builds up between the green leaf and the soil. Once it gets past about 15 mm it starts shedding water and hiding pests. A scarifier, also called a dethatcher or verti-mower, cuts vertically through that layer and lifts it out.

Corer or Scarifier? Pick The Right One.

Hiring the wrong machine is the most common mistake we see. They solve two completely different problems, and running a scarifier over compacted soil will not fix compaction.

Hire a corer if…

Water pools or runs off instead of soaking in. The ground is hard to push a screwdriver into. The lawn browns off first in high-traffic strips. You have clay or heavily compacted sand.

→ Core aeration, then topdress.

Hire a scarifier if…

The lawn feels spongy or bouncy underfoot. There is a thick brown mat under the green. Mower wheels sink in. New growth sits on top of old runners rather than in soil.

→ Scarify or verti-mow, then fertilise.

Hire Questions

What does dry hire mean?

Dry hire means you take the machine and operate it yourself. We supply the equipment, show you how it runs and what settings suit your grass type, and you do the work in your own time. If you would rather we ran the machine for you, that is our standard renovation service.

Do I need a licence or trailer to hire a lawn aerator?

No licence is required. These are walk-behind petrol machines. You will need a vehicle capable of transporting the machine safely, or ask us about transport when you book.

Should I hire a corer or a scarifier?

They fix different problems. A corer or core aerator pulls plugs out of the soil to relieve compaction and let water and air reach the roots. A scarifier or verti-mower cuts vertically through the thatch layer sitting above the soil. Compacted, water-repellent ground wants a corer. A spongy lawn that feels thick underfoot wants a scarifier.

How much does lawn aerator hire cost in Perth?

Rates depend on the machine and how long you need it. Call 0427 206 246 and we will quote the machine that suits your lawn size and soil, or use the full pricing calculator.

Can I hire a scarifier for buffalo lawn?

Yes, but set the blades shallower than you would for couch. Buffalo (Sir Walter, Palmetto, Sapphire) is a surface runner and does not tolerate being cut right to the soil. We will set the depth for you before you leave with the machine.

When is the best time to hire?

Spring through early summer, once the soil has warmed and the lawn is actively growing. Renovation work done in the growing season recovers in weeks; the same work done heading into winter just leaves the lawn thin and open. Hire gear books out fastest in spring, so call early.
