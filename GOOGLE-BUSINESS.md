# Google Business Profile: Perth Lawn Renovation Services

Everything to paste in, tab by tab, matching the **Business information** dialog.
All copy is written and ready. Tick each off as you go.

Your Google Business Profile drives the **Map Pack** (the 3 businesses shown with
the map). That is where the phone calls come from, and it matters more than the
website for local searches. Fill in every field: Google ranks complete profiles
above incomplete ones.

## Read this first: your categories look wrong

The **More** tab is currently offering you *Drive-through*, *Happy hours*,
*Kitchen*, *Lunch*, *Takeaway* and *Pick-up*. Those are food and hospitality
attributes. Google only shows attributes that match your **category**, so if
you are seeing them, one of your categories is set to a restaurant or food type.

**Fix the categories first (About tab, below), then come back to More.** Once
the category is right, those food attributes disappear and the useful ones
(*Online estimates*, *On-site services*, *Appointment required*) appear instead.

Do not tick any of the food attributes. Leave every one of them blank.

### 3. Reviews (this is the actual ranking fuel)

The Map Pack order is driven heavily by **number of reviews, recency and rating**.
A competitor with 40 reviews will beat a better business with 3.

- [ ] Get your short review link: **Edit profile → Ask for reviews → copy link**.
      Save it as a text shortcut on your phone.
- [ ] Text it after **every** job:

```
Thanks again for having us out! If you're happy with the lawn, a quick Google review would genuinely help a small local business. Takes 30 seconds: [link]
```

- [ ] Aim for 1 to 2 new reviews a week. That pace alone moves you up.
- [ ] Reply to every single review, even a one-word one. Replies are a ranking signal.
- [ ] Never buy or fake reviews. Google removes them and can suspend the profile,
      and fake reviews breach Australian Consumer Law.

### 4. Posts and Q&A

- [ ] Post a weekly **Update**: one job photo and one line of text. Two minutes,
      and it keeps the profile active, which Google rewards.
- [ ] Seed your own **Q&A**. You are allowed to ask and answer your own questions,
      and it fills the space before someone else asks something awkward:
