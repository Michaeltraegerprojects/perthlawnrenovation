Your lawn
aftercare guide

Your lawn has just been renovated. What you do over the next three weeks decides how thick and green it comes back. Follow this guide and you will be mowing a better lawn within two to three weeks.

1The first 3 weeks, week by week

Recovery times vary with the weather and how healthy your lawn was to start with. This is the typical pattern after vertimowing, dethatching or coring.

Days 1 to 3
Water deeply, twice a day. Short bursts are not enough. Water early morning and late afternoon so the soil stays damp through the root zone. Keep off the lawn where you can.
Days 4 to 7
Do not panic at the look. Short, scalped and bare is completely normal after dethatching. The runners are alive under the surface and pushing new growth.
Week 2
Feed it. Apply a quality lawn fertiliser and keep watering. This is when you will see fresh green shoots filling the gaps. Back watering off to once a day if the weather is mild.
Weeks 2 to 3
First mow. Once the new growth is about 30mm, mow on a high setting and only take the top off. Do not scalp it. Leave the catcher on for the first two cuts.
Week 4 onward
Back to normal. Return to your usual mowing height and schedule. Keep a wetting agent going through summer so water actually soaks into Perth's sandy soils.

2Do and do not

Do

  • Water deeply and consistently, especially the first week
  • Apply fertiliser around week two
  • Use a wetting agent on sandy or hydrophobic soil
  • Mow high for the first two cuts
  • Check your reticulation is covering evenly

Do not

  • Do not let the lawn dry out in the first fortnight
  • Do not scalp it on the first mow
  • Do not apply weed killer for at least three weeks
  • Do not park cars or stack materials on new growth
  • Do not worry about the bare look, it fills in
Watering rule of thumb: damp soil 50mm down is the target. Push a screwdriver into the lawn. If it slides in easily, moisture is getting through. If it stops short, you need a longer run or a wetting agent.

4Your seasonal calendar

Spring
Renovate, feed, wetting agent. Prime growing window in Perth.
Summer
Wetting agent monthly, feed lightly, mow high to shade the soil.
Autumn
Feed, treat weeds, last chance renovation before mid April.
Winter
Ease off water, treat wintergrass, plan your spring renovation.
